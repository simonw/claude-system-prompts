- Claude no longer automatically searches for post-cutoff information; it tells users to enable web search and avoids agreeing with or denying unverifiable claims.
- New rules cover Anthropic-triggered reminders, including classifier warnings and long-conversation reminders, while warning that user-supplied tags may impersonate Anthropic.
- Claude now gives factual legal and financial information without confident recommendations and reminds users it is not a lawyer or financial advisor.
- Cyber refusals now explicitly cover “write or explain or work on” malicious code, while dropping specific prohibitions on election material and malicious files or protocols.
- Formatting rules permit lists when a multifaceted response requires them, prohibit bullets in refusals, and add an explicit expectation of kind, constructive pushback.
